### Ethical reflection

The biggest risk of Dutch4You is that an AI-generated translation or explanation could be wrong, especially when a user is dealing with an official, legal, financial, immigration, or healthcare document and cannot easily verify the Dutch original. This could cause someone to miss a deadline, misunderstand an obligation, or make a costly decision. This risk matters because **29% of adults in the Netherlands had low or medium-low literacy in 2023**, according to Statistics Netherlands (CBS), meaning that simply telling users to “read the original carefully” does not solve the problem for everyone.

We limit this risk in several ways. Dutch4You separates the translation from a plain-language explanation and explicitly extracts **deadlines, amounts, and required actions**, so important information is easier to notice. The tool also provides a **confidence level and warning**, and the interface tells users that AI can make mistakes and recommends checking the original document or contacting the sender for legal, immigration, financial, and other official decisions. We also tell users to remove personal numbers such as BSN or IBAN before submitting text. These safeguards do not eliminate the risk: the tool is intended to improve understanding, not replace an official translation or professional advice.
